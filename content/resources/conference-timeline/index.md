This page summarizes major AI/ML conference timelines.  
Dates not yet officially announced are estimated based on recent (2023–2025) patterns.

---

### Submission Deadlines (Chronological Order)

- AAAI — July 25 [Abstract], August 1 [Full], 2025
- ICLR — September 19 [Abstract], September 24 [Full], 2025
- CVPR — November 7 [Abstract], November 13 [Full], 2025
- ACL — January 5, 2026
- IJCAI — January 12 [Abstract], January 19 [Full], 2026
- ICML — January 23 [Abstract], January 28 [Full], 2026
- AISTATS — January 31, 2026
- ECCV (even years) — February 26 [Reg.], March 5 [Full], 2026
- NeurIPS — Mid May, 2026 [Tentative]
- EMNLP — May 25, 2026
- ICCV (odd years) — March, 2027 [Tentative]
