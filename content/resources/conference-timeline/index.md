This page summarizes major AI/ML conference timelines.  
Dates not yet officially announced are estimated based on recent (2023–2025) patterns.

---

### Submission Deadlines (Chronological Order)

- ACL — January
- IJCAI — January
- ICML — Late January / Early February
- ICCV (odd years) — March
- ECCV (even years) — March
- NeurIPS — Mid May
- EMNLP — June
- AAAI — August
- AISTATS — October
- ICLR — Late September
- CVPR — November

---

### Paper Release / Conference Periods (Chronological Order)

- AAAI — February
- AISTATS — April / May
- ICLR — May
- CVPR — June
- ICML — July
- ACL — July / August
- IJCAI — August
- ECCV — September
- ICCV — October
- EMNLP — November / December
- NeurIPS — December

---

### Notes

- Exact dates vary slightly each year.
- Many conferences have an abstract deadline approximately one week before the full paper deadline.
- This page is intended as a planning reference for the NIU AI/ML community.
