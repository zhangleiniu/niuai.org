This page summarizes major AI/ML conference timelines for the 2026–2027 cycle.  
Dates not yet officially announced are estimated from previous years and marked [Tentative].  
Deadlines are Anywhere on Earth (AoE) unless the conference says otherwise.  
*Last updated: November 18, 2026.*

---

- AAAI-27 — July 21 [Abstract], July 28 [Full], 2026 [Passed]
- ICLR 2027 — September 18 [Abstract], September 25 [Full], 2026 [Passed]
- AISTATS 2027 — September 29 [Abstract], October 6 [Full], 2026 [Passed]
- CVPR 2027 — November 10 [Abstract], November 16 [Full], 2026 [Passed]
- ACL 2027 — January 4, 2027 [ARR submission]
- IJCAI 2027 — January 4 [Abstract], January 11 [Full], 2027 [Tentative]
- ICML 2027 — January 14 [Abstract], January 22 [Full], 2027
- ICCV 2027 (odd years) — Early March, 2027 [Tentative]

### Notes

- Exact dates vary slightly each year.
- Many conferences have an abstract deadline approximately one week before the full paper deadline.
- ACL and EMNLP take submissions through ACL Rolling Review (ARR); check the [ARR dates page](https://aclrollingreview.org/dates) for each cycle.
- This page is intended as a planning reference for the NIU AI/ML community.
