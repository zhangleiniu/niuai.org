## Overview

**Speaker:** Ibrahim Al Azher  
**Date:** Friday, September 18, 2026, 2:30 PM  
**Room:** PM103

This session covers adversarial attacks on language models and the defenses against them, organized in three parts: attacks, defenses, and tool-based attack and defense. The reading list for each part is below.
