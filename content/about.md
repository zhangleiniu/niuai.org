## Our Mission

The NIU AI Research Group is dedicated to exploring artificial intelligence through collaborative learning, student presentations, and innovative research projects.

- **Student Presentations**: Regular presentations where students share their research and insights
- **Research Projects**: Collaborative initiatives exploring AI applications and education
- **Community Building**: Creating a supportive environment for AI learning
- **Educational Outreach**: Extending AI education beyond the university
